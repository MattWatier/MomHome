  <h1>Care lens</h1>
  <p class="lede">Shared criteria for the family shortlist. No personal medical or financial details live on this site.</p>

  <h2>Search anchor</h2>
  <p><strong>Vienna Metro</strong> (“close to our house”) — parking lot at 9493 Virginia Center Blvd, Vienna, VA 22181. Radiate outward in Virginia; avoid Maryland when possible because of travel-time compounding.</p>

  <h2>What we’re looking for</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Assisted living (not memory care / nursing home unless continuum matters later)</li>
    <li>Three meals prepared each day</li>
    <li>Meds: reminder / self-administer with oversight</li>
    <li>Mobility: walks with cane; walk-in shower; no transfers</li>
    <li>Private room; no pets; non-smoker</li>
    <li>Budget: comfortable under ~$5k/mo all-in; stretch up to ~$8k</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>How cards work</h2>
  <p>Location cards show the researched <strong>all-in monthly estimate</strong>. Open a location for the price breakdown, seed notes, review sentiment, and source links. Early numbers may be web-sourced; we refine after visits.</p>

  <h2>Before tours</h2>
  <ol>
    <li>VA DSS assisted living inspections</li>
    <li>VA Dept of Health kitchen inspections by county</li>
    <li>Google + Yelp (with source URLs)</li>
    <li>Staff picture via Glassdoor / LinkedIn public profiles</li>
    <li>Then schedule meetings</li>
  </ol>
