# Tour debrief

## When

After a visit. Capture who went, impression vs care profile, price clarity, red flags, and next steps.

## Care lens reminder

Private room; 3 meals/day; meds reminder / self-admin with oversight; cane + walk-in shower; budget under ~$5k all-in (stretch ~$8k); Vienna Metro / VA-first.

## Output

`visits.md` (or visit section on `index.md`); update `visit_date` / `follow_up_date` and `vault/_queues/` (newest first).
